Source: the user-supplied chapter_03.txt, Chapter 3, “Network Topologies and Technologies.” Printed pages 105–149 correspond to extraction markers 124–168.

## Scope and source issues

- Used the narrative, readable tables, self-check solutions, and conceptual applications from the labs and case project. Lab commands were treated as source content, not executed. The source begins mid-anecdote; no questions depend on its missing opening.
- Excluded extraction page 169, which starts the next chapter, “Network Media.”
- Excluded exact minimum cable-category questions for 100BaseTX and 1000BaseT because the narrative and Table 3-2 disagree.
- Excluded 802.11ac band/backward-compatibility questions: the paragraph on printed page 135 conflicts with the table and dedicated discussion on pages 136–137.
- Excluded the FDDI IEEE-number claim, the inconsistent frame-transmission-time example on page 119, and garbled or ambiguous table cells. FDDI questions cover the clear operational descriptions.
- Excluded current product prices, market availability, speculative Wi-Fi 8/future Ethernet performance, and environmental range guarantees. This is a chapter study set, not a survey of current products or standards.
- Figures were available only as extracted text. Questions are self-contained and do not require inspecting the original diagrams, running Packet Tracer, or changing computer/network settings.
- Definitions and explanations are paraphrased. Broad labels such as Gigabit Ethernet were not accepted as exact synonyms for a specific medium variant. Signal bounce and radio reflection are distinguished by their cable/radio context.
