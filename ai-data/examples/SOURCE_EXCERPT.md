## Network scope

A local area network (LAN) covers a small geographic area, such as one building. A wide area network (WAN) spans long geographic distances and can connect offices in distant cities.

## Connecting networks

A router is a device that forwards packets between IP networks. Routers can forward traffic between the IP networks of geographically separated offices connected through a WAN.

## Data units

Eight bits form one byte.
